# Weapon, element, technique and build coverage: v2.0.11 research report

This is a design investigation for the next implementer, not a content manifest or proof that every build is balanced. Reproduce it against the actual checkout; inspect the engine and disagree where evidence supports a better choice. The detailed Thai catalog tables are in [WEAPON_PATH_COVERAGE_AUDIT_V2_0_11.md](./WEAPON_PATH_COVERAGE_AUDIT_V2_0_11.md); every major stage of every current path/subpath has a reproducible diagnostic row in [weapon_coverage_v211.csv](./docs/audits/weapon_coverage_v211.csv). Regenerate with `node docs/audits/weapon_coverage_snapshot.js > /tmp/jhevo-coverage.csv` from the repository root.

## Definitions, evidence, and limits

The runtime snapshot loads `GAME_SOURCE/test/load.js`. It contains 159 `JD.ITEMS.items` WEAPON records across the seven named gear types, 638 `JD.TECH.techniques` records, and 78 path/subpath entries. Counts refer to definitions, not guaranteed loot or actual usage; aliases and additional dynamic/generated or recipe-only equipment can live elsewhere. The 78 is a snapshot, not a target or restriction on future design. The 159 weapons per type are: SWORD 24, BLADE 26, SPEAR 23, STAFF 22, FIST 20, BOW 21, FOCUS 23. Every type has at least two entries in each catalog tier 1–7. This does **not** guarantee an accessible, elemental upgrade at every cultivation stage.

Weapon `tier`, technique `tier`/`reqTier`, technique `grade`, gear instance quality `q`, spiritual-root grade, cultivation stage, specialization track ordinal and equivalent stage are distinct concepts. In this snapshot 148 of the 159 catalog weapons do not have a `grade` field, and 11 have `grade: Legendary`; do not interpret missing grade as common. `ECO.mintGear` uses a numeric quality `q`; crafting and refinement can change it. Technique grades are Common 100, Refined 139, Rare 199, Exceptional 186, Legendary 14, counted across the 638 technique records. The Thai audit includes a weapon-type × technique-grade × reqTier matrix. The implementer must establish how weapon grade/rarity is actually expressed or derived before claiming a complete weapon grade matrix.

For the 572-row snapshot, each subpath is evaluated at every one of its own major stages using `CULT.maxStage`, `CULT.equivalentStage`, and `CULT.effectiveTechStage`. The fixture uses one seeded starter NPC, clears learned techniques and specialization track, makes the whole technique catalog hypothetically available, and applies `CULT.learnable` with default options. It does not enable forbidden study, simulate acquiring books, include innate abilities in learnable counts, vary NPC personality, test actual combat, model resources or time, or prove AI decisions. Its gear filter mirrors the initial stage/path checks of `MARTIAL.gearSuitability` (`minStage <= effectiveTechStage + 1`), then requires a preferred weapon type and primary element in the subpath profile. Counts of matching techniques require a preferred `weaponTypes` entry and matching **primary** element. Weighted secondary elements, neutral techniques/equipment, untyped weapon-unrestricted techniques, alternate weapons, innate moves, crafted-only or other data sources may offer useful options and are not counted as matches. Rows are an optimistic *availability-of-definitions* upper bound, not proof of obtainable builds; zeros are a prioritization signal, not a hard lockout.

The primary and secondary elements used for the subpath are the elements in that subpath's runtime `elementProfile.weights`, not a restriction on a character's possible spiritual roots or builds. `MARTIAL.weaponCompatibility` distinguishes matched, adapted, severe mismatch and blocked required weapon. `MARTIAL.resolveTechCompatibility` additionally changes study speed and combat power for path/subpath affinity. A technically learnable cross-path manual is therefore not necessarily equivalent in combat, and not every explicit `compatibleSubs` entry proves actual access. `CULT.learnable` considers the supplied library, while the AI in `js/ai.js` consults the sect's actual library and additional caps. Check innate, exclusive/forbidden, legendary, passive and unrestricted-weapon rules separately.

## Priority findings and provisional additions

At stage 1, the diagnostic criterion finds zero preferred-weapon / matching-primary-element catalog gear for XIAN `SOUND_ZITHER` (FOCUS; WIND/LIGHT_YANG), GOD `UNDERWORLD_JUDGE` (STAFF/FOCUS; YIN_SHADOW/FIRE), and HEART_SOUL `DREAM_ILLUSION_WEAVER` (FOCUS; YIN_SHADOW/WIND). Seven subpaths have zero learnable preferred-weapon / matching-primary-element techniques under the all-library fixture: BODY `THUNDER_BODY`, GOD `UNDERWORLD_JUDGE`, GOD `SUN_BOW_DEITY`, DEMON `NETHER_GHOST_KING`, DEMON `BLOOD_SPEAR_ASURA`, HEART_SOUL `VOID_SPEAR_MIND`, and KARMA_SAMSARA `ASURA_JUDGMENT_BLADE`. At each path's maximum stage this particular diagnostic finds at least one gear definition and one learnable matching technique for every subpath; that is not evidence of adequate choice, acquisition, combat parity, or upgrade continuity between stages.

Candidate work, subject to verification and existing-content reuse:

1. **Early access first.** Consider low-stage wind/yang FOCUS for zither, yin/fire FOCUS or STAFF for the Underworld Judge, and yin/wind FOCUS for Dream Illusion. Add or make discoverable early compatible lightning SPEAR/FIST, light/fire BOW, yin/water FOCUS/STAFF, blood/fire SPEAR/BLADE, astral/yin SPEAR/STAFF and fire/blood BLADE/SWORD/SPEAR techniques where the seven flagged builds lack genuine alternatives. A shared manual may serve several concepts when its effects, costs and affinities make sense; use exclusivity only where identity or balance justifies it.
2. **Endgame progression.** The base catalog has T7 counts of 2 STAFF, 2 FIST, 2 BOW, 3 SPEAR, 3 FOCUS, versus 4 SWORD and 4 BLADE; preferred-element coverage is sparser than the type totals. Inspect actual acquisition and stage requirements before adding a distinct mid/late upgrade for an underserved build. Preserve existing T7 items and meaningful neutral alternatives.
3. **Element × weapon priorities.** No primary WATER_ICE BLADE/BOW, WIND STAFF/FIST/FOCUS, WOOD_LIFE SWORD/SPEAR/FIST/BOW, YIN_SHADOW SPEAR/STAFF/FIST/BOW, LIGHTNING FOCUS, or BLOOD_DEMON SPEAR/STAFF/BOW/FOCUS appears in the counted catalog. These are candidate gaps, not a requirement to manufacture every cell. Check weighted secondary elements, recipe-only outputs, lore, rarity and actual demand first. Focus on combinations justified by several real subpaths or a compelling unique build.
4. **Shared versus specialized content.** Design useful neutral/cross-path foundations for training and transitions; add path/subpath-specific moves, passives or signature weapons when their gameplay creates meaningful identity. An attainable setup with matched weapon compatibility and coherent actions matters more than identical catalog counts. Check combat and support roles separately; a healer need not match an assassin's raw damage.
5. **Acquisition and autonomy.** Trace each proposed bridge to at least one credible source at the intended stage (starting archetype, learnable library/manual discovery, crafting recipe and materials, market, expedition or rewards). Verify `ECO.itemById`, recipe output IDs, catalogs and NPC study/equipment choices. A recipe existing or an editor spawn working alone does not prove normal-play availability.
6. **Improvisation beyond the listed gaps.** Independently invent and evaluate additional interactions that let NPCs adapt their build or tactics to the actual situation: viable neutral or cross-element fallbacks, alternate weapon choices, role-appropriate support, complementary team loadouts and pet/technique coordination. Ground choices in known information, personality, resources and existing mechanics. Test changes in NPC behavior, not just the existence of catalog entries, and avoid making all NPCs converge on the same build.

## Per-major-path design leads

These are testable hypotheses, not prescriptive quotas. Use the CSV to inspect each subpath and each intermediate stage; check the Thai report for the exact profile/weapon/tier distributions.

- **XIAN (13 snapshot entries):** `SOUND_ZITHER` starts with no matched-primary-element FOCUS in the sampled gear catalog. Investigate WIND/LIGHT_YANG focus instruments, shared sound-control techniques and later alternatives for `TIME_VOID`, `YIN_YANG_HARMONY`, frost sword and cloud spear. Ensure cross-path variants do not erase identity.
- **BODY (13):** `THUNDER_BODY` has no sampled stage-1 preferred-weapon LIGHTNING technique. Evaluate a low-stage unarmed/fist or spear lightning training foundation and high-stage progressions; check WOOD_LIFE FIST options for wood/poison bodies, and preserve defensive/physical roles.
- **GOD (10):** `UNDERWORLD_JUDGE` is flagged on both starting gear and technique; `SUN_BOW_DEITY` on starting matching technique. `OCEAN_SOVEREIGN` has 3 matched catalog weapons across tiers 2 and 4 and 6 explicitly associated techniques at reqTiers 1, 4, 7. Investigate tide/wind SPEAR/FOCUS and solar bow bridges, including access before low-stage disciples can afford specialty items.
- **DEMON (7):** `NETHER_GHOST_KING` and `BLOOD_SPEAR_ASURA` lack an early matching-primary-element learnable technique in the sample. Consider yin/water FOCUS/STAFF and blood/fire SPEAR/BLADE early foundations, then differentiate control, cost and forbidden mechanics; check toxin/gu identity and counterplay, not just power.
- **HEART_SOUL (7):** `DREAM_ILLUSION_WEAVER` starts with no counted matching FOCUS gear; `VOID_SPEAR_MIND` lacks a matching stage-1 technique. Consider wind/yin focus and astral/yin spear options, with mental/control or defensive roles and realistic acquisition.
- **MECH_PUPPET (7):** Each subpath has only 3–6 explicitly tagged technique definitions in the snapshot; ordinary cross-path manuals may be available. Check construct, thread, ballista and orbiting-blade roles across stages and the existing puppet/construct actions before adding control manuals or gear. Do not infer absence merely from small tagged counts.
- **KARMA_SAMSARA (7):** Explicit tags number 3–6 per subpath; `ASURA_JUDGMENT_BLADE` has no early matching technique under this fixture. Test fire/blood melee bridges alongside causality, judgment, protection and vow roles. Account for path-stage equivalence and avoid forcing a single damage-oriented identity.
- **BEAST_SOVEREIGN (7):** Explicit tags range 2–6. `SWARM_GU_HIVE_MASTER` has two explicit techniques at reqTiers 1 and 3, although many shared FOCUS techniques pass learnable checks when all books are granted. Investigate wood/yin FOCUS/BOW/BLADE, pet command, bonded defense and late-stage pet synergies; don't require a pet for unrelated paths or count pet power twice.
- **SAGE_SUPPORT (7):** Explicit tags range 2–3. `CELESTIAL_ZITHER_ENVOY` has two tagged techniques at reqTiers 2 and 3, yet shared techniques exist in the all-library fixture. Investigate attainable early/mid/late healing, warding, buff and resource-efficient support options for needle, zither, alchemy, talisman, lantern, banner and wood symbiosis. Evaluate team outcomes, opportunity cost and survival rather than requiring DPS parity.

The diagnostic tagged counts are repeated if a technique declares multiple compatible subpaths, may include content subject to further restrictions, and must not be summed as unique manual counts. For example, the all-library stage-1 / middle / maximum eligibility sample for solar-bow is 3 / 11 / 18 preferred-weapon techniques, but matching fire/yang primary-element counts are 0 / 2 / 5. Those are still hypothetical library access, not observed play.

## Before declaring parity

Run representative full builds from early/middle/late cultivation in multiple paths, roles and weapons, including the seven flagged subpaths and at least one alternative build per major path. Identify actual weapon/manual IDs, how obtained, effective stages, compatible moves and element interactions, status/support functionality, costs, RNG and AI choices. Sample seeded autonomous play or representative combat/support scenarios where feasible; compare results under comparable investment, level and enemy conditions. Report distribution and methodology, not an unsupported blanket claim of balance. Check healing/utility, crowd control, survival, individual and party outcomes as appropriate. Recheck gear/tech acquisition, difficulty, progression pacing, save/load and performance; new content should not create infinite-resource or compounding-stat loops. Record remaining uncertainty and specific next work.
