### Procedurally attuned common and mid-tier equipment

Evaluate a **reusable equipment-family approach** so the project does not need a separately authored item for every weapon-type × element × grade combination. One suitable direction is for eligible common, craftable or discovered weapons to roll an elemental attunement and a bounded item tier/quality when minted. For example, a generic sword pattern could produce a fire, water, wind, wood, lightning, neutral or other valid attunement when the source and lore allow it. This is a design direction to validate and refine, not permission to randomize every existing item.

Keep the random pool mainly in low-to-mid equipment tiers (provisionally through T3–T4); first verify what the player's “grade 3–4” means in the actual runtime vocabulary (`tier`, `grade`, rarity or numeric `q`) and use the existing system correctly. Preserve named signature weapons, unique/legendary rewards, fixed lore artifacts and resonance-set pieces unless evidence supports a carefully bounded variant system. Roll all related properties together so names, `element`, `elementProfile`, stats/effects, recipes, market/drop display, NPC evaluation and save/load agree. Keep grade/tier, rarity and numeric quality as separate concepts; don't silently make higher random rolls multiply item power beyond existing budgets.

The desired element may be **more likely** when supported by the source (cultivator's affinity, craft specialist, region, recipe or event), while remaining a genuine chance with no guarantee that a particular NPC obtains the wanted result. Determine and explain sensible weights from evidence; do not give NPCs a hidden target-aware roll unavailable to the player. Ensure every valid build has a plausible non-random fallback (neutral, cross-element, alternate weapon, compatible manual or craft route) so a bad roll cannot permanently block progression. Avoid unlimited rerolls, free targeted crafting, hidden pity that guarantees a requested element, and exploit loops. Use the game's seeded RNG, preserve source-appropriate element pools, and test distribution, acquisition, tier/quality bounds, actual equip/technique compatibility, display, deterministic saves and economy over many generated samples. If the best design differs from this example, implement the better justified design and document the trade-off.

Design and **implement** an appropriately scoped, coherent set of useful weapons, techniques, progression links, recipes, discovery routes and balance adjustments. Shared/unrestricted manuals and equipment are welcome when they form meaningful cross-path options; restricted techniques, subpath signatures and special weapon effects are welcome when they establish identity. Choose intentionally, explain trade-offs, and ensure that the content is actually connected to the game's data indices, effects, learning, equipping, AI, editor and acquisition systems. Record each new content family's source, gating and approximate availability in the implementation report, and add meaningful tests that exercise representative acquisition-to-use chains. Editor spawning and the presence of a recipe alone do not establish normal-play accessibility.
