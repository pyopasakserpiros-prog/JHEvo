# v2.0.24 — Living World Atlas

Based on v2.0.23 (`cd617c5`). The old crowded map was replaced with an illustrated, navigable map of the five existing macro-regions. The terrain is embedded in the generated standalone HTML so it also works offline. The map can be dragged and zoomed by buttons, wheel, or two-finger pinch. Nearby destinations form selectable groups; category filters and zoom-dependent labels keep the overview readable. Faction colors and individual emblems distinguish the 19 existing sects. A nonmodal detail panel leaves the surrounding map usable.

The atlas reads the actual world topology and state rather than maintaining a second world. It includes the player's sect, all 19 outside sects, 12 expedition regions, six strategic resources, the trade hub, four sanctums, the pagoda, five rotating elemental realms, 20 fortune sites, six secret realm sites, living wandering legends, active timed anomalies, vassal distress calls, and the dimensional gate. The 16 worlds beyond the gate remain separate dimensions, accessed through the gate inside the sect. Living events, ownership, faction alignment, and available actions reflect their current simulation state. The six newer expedition regions now have positions matched to their described terrain instead of sharing a single point. Fortune sites, secret realms, and wandering legends have region-level information in the simulation; their map pins explicitly say the exact position is approximate.

Players can send actual expeditions to explore regions and an explicitly chosen fortune site, travel to available sanctums or trials, contest an anomaly, attack a sect, or claim a resource. A resource claim now travels first and can fail against its defending garrison; it no longer has a guaranteed victory when the expedition resolves. The map uses the same selected party and calculation as the corresponding expedition. Randomized expedition durations are displayed as a range; sanctum and trial estimates follow those systems' actual timing. Duplicate simultaneous attacks or claims on the same target are rejected.

Two related bugs were repaired: an anomaly that expired while a party was travelling could leave the party away indefinitely, and pagoda/elemental-realm missions lacked labels needed by the activity and travel screens. The travel list now shows actual destination names, including resource points and chosen fortune sites.
